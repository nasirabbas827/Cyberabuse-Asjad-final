About CyberGuard

This project leverages machine learning models — Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, and SVM — to analyze and classify Roman Urdu text. Our goal is to empower online platforms to automatically detect and flag harmful content, helping to create a more positive and respectful digital environment.
